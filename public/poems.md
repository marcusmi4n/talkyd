# Five Poems

## 1. The Quiet Morning

The sun rises soft through the mist,
A golden thread through gray,
The world still sleeps in quiet bliss,
At breaking of the day.

The birds begin their gentle song,
The dew sits on the grass,
The night retreats, no more belongs,
As morning comes to pass.

---

## 2. Autumn's Farewell

The leaves turn gold and crimson bright,
Before they fall to ground,
A final dance in fading light,
Before the snow comes down.

The air grows crisp, the days grow short,
The sun hangs low and pale,
Autumn's last and loving court,
Before the winter gale.

---

## 3. The Old Bookshop

Between these walls of yellowed pages,
Where time moves slow and sweet,
The wisdom lives through all the ages,
In rows from floor to seat.

The smell of paper, worn and old,
A story on each shelf,
A thousand worlds here to unfold,
For those who seek themselves.

---

## 4. River's Journey

From mountain spring to ocean wide,
The river makes its way,
Through valley deep and mountainside,
By night and into day.

It carves the stone with patient touch,
It feeds the thirsty land,
It gives to all, it asks not much,
As it follows nature's plan.

---

## 5. The Lighthouse Keeper

Upon the cliff where waves crash high,
The lighthouse stands alone,
A beacon in the stormy sky,
For sailors far from home.

The keeper tends the burning light,
Through longest, darkest night,
A guardian against the fright,
Until the morning bright.

---
